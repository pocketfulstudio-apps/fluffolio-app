Privacy / 隐私

Updated November 5, 2026. Fluffolio is a journal and scrapbook app in development by Pocketful Studio. This notice describes the development app and this product website. New cloud and collaboration features are still under testing.

On your device

Plushie profiles, birthdays, memories, selected photos, transparent stickers and editable scrapbook pages are stored in the app’s local library. You choose which photos to import. Local background removal uses Apple’s on-device subject processing on supported iPhones. It does not send the source photo to a cutout server. Local reminders use the system notification service when you allow them. Locations in memories are entered by you; the app does not continuously track your location.

Accounts and cloud features

Account authentication and cloud storage use Supabase. Google sign-in is connected in the development app; Apple sign-in is being prepared. The login provider may share your account identifier, name and email according to the choices in its sign-in screen. Passwords or provider credentials are not displayed to other users.

Optional backup uploads this device’s local library, including selected original images, transparent stickers and editable project files, to your account’s private storage. It requires an explicit opt-in. Signing in by itself does not upload your full photo library. Switching accounts does not transfer private backups. Backup and restore are still being validated in development builds.

Shared memories

Friends can see the posts you choose to share with them. A couple or family space is visible to its accepted members, including older content in that space. Editors can change shared records; viewers can read them. Shared scrapbook draft synchronization is under testing. Removing a member revokes cloud access, but cannot recall copies or exports already saved to someone’s device.

Reports and blocks are used to handle unwanted content and interactions. Support correspondence may include the information you choose to email us. Do not send passwords, access keys or sensitive identity documents.

Retention and deletion

Local data stays on the device until you remove it or remove the app. Keep a verified backup before changing phones or uninstalling. Cloud account data remains while your account and its cloud content are retained.

Request account deletion in Me → Privacy & account → Request cloud account deletion, or contact pocketfulstudio.apps@gmail.com. Deletion is reviewed manually. Our target is completion within 7 days; if verification or more time is needed, we will contact your account email and confirm completion. Contact support to withdraw a pending request.

Deletion covers your login account, profile, friend posts, private backups and associated cloud photos and stickers. Transfer administration of shared spaces with other members first. Collaborative work belonging to those spaces is retained, with your personal account attribution removed. Your device’s local journal is not remotely erased. Provider recovery backups and required security records may persist until their normal expiry.

Website and providers

This website does not use advertising trackers, analytics scripts, forms that store submissions, or cookies set by us. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; the hosting provider may process technical request logs. Email links open your mail app and send only if you choose to send. Supabase, Google and GitHub operate their services under their own privacy policies. Their infrastructure may process information outside your country. We do not sell journal content or use it for advertising.

Contact

For access, correction, deletion or privacy questions, email pocketfulstudio.apps@gmail.com.

中文隐私说明

更新于 2026 年 11 月 5 日。Fluffolio 由 Pocketful Studio 开发，尚未公开发布。新增云备份和共同创作功能仍在测试。

玩偶档案、生日、手账、你选择的照片、透明贴纸和剪贴簿工程默认保存在设备上。照片由你选取导入；支持的 iPhone 在本机进行抠图，不发送到抠图服务器。提醒需你允许通知。地点由你填写，App 不持续追踪位置。

登录和云端数据使用 Supabase。开发版已接入 Google 登录；Apple 登录正在准备。登录提供方按授权页面提供身份标识、姓名和邮箱。其他用户看不到你的密码或登录凭据。

云备份需要主动开启，会上传这台设备的本机资料，包括你选择的原图、贴纸和工程，归属于当前账号的私人空间。仅登录不会上传整个系统相册，换账号不会转移私人备份。备份恢复仍在验收。

主动分享的帖子对相应娃友可见。情侣或家庭空间的内容对已接受邀请的成员可见，包括之前的内容；编辑成员可修改，只读成员可查看。移除成员会撤销云端访问，但无法收回对方已下载或导出的副本。举报与屏蔽用于处理不当内容。

本机资料保存到你删除内容或卸载 App 为止；换机或卸载前请确认备份已完成。云端资料在账号及内容保留期间保存。

可在「我的 → 隐私与账号 → 申请删除云端账号」发起申请，也可联系工作室邮箱。由运营人工核实处理，目标 7 天内完成；如需进一步核实或更多时间，将通过账号邮箱联系，并确认完成。撤回申请请联系支持。

删除范围包括登录账号、个人资料、娃友分享、私人备份及其云端照片和贴纸。有其他成员的共同空间请先转交管理员；该空间共同作品保留，移除个人账号关联。本机手账不会被远程清除。服务商恢复备份及必要安全记录可能保留至正常到期。

本网站不设置广告追踪、分析脚本、存储提交内容的表单或自有 Cookie，使用 GitHub Pages 托管，服务商可能处理技术访问日志。邮件链接只打开你的邮件 App，由你决定是否发送。Supabase、Google、GitHub 各自按其隐私政策运营，基础设施可能在境外处理资料。我们不出售手账内容，也不将其用于广告。

隐私、查询、更正或删除问题，请联系 pocketfulstudio.apps@gmail.com。请勿发送密码、密钥或敏感身份证明。

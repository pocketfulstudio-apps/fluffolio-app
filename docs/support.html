Support / 支持

Fluffolio is an iPhone plushie journal and scrapbook in development. There is no public App Store or TestFlight link yet.

Email Pocketful Studio

A photo or draft is missing

Keep the app installed and avoid clearing its data. Check the account and shared space you are viewing. Personal local memories belong to this device; signing into another account does not move them. If you enabled cloud backup, check the last completed backup before restoring. A restore adds copies and keeps existing memories.

Sticker cutouts

Background removal needs a supported iPhone and iOS 17 or later. Use a clear, well-lit photo with space around the plushie. Check fur edges on both light and dark backgrounds. Subject selection and the new editor are still being tested on physical devices. The simulator does not support the cutout feature.

Couple and family spaces

Each person uses their own account and username. The space administrator sends an invitation; the other person accepts it in their own account. Couple spaces hold two members, family spaces up to ten. Members see the whole shared space. An administrator can manage roles and transfer ownership.

Account deletion

In the app, open Me → Privacy & account to register a manual deletion request. You can also email us from the email associated with your account. We aim to process requests within 7 days, contact you if further verification is needed, and confirm completion. Do not send a password. Read the scope in our privacy policy.

What to include

Tell us your app version, iPhone model, iOS version and what you were doing when the issue happened. A screenshot is helpful if you remove private photos, email addresses and other personal details first.

中文支持

Fluffolio 是正在开发的玩偶手账与剪贴簿 App，暂无公开 App Store 或 TestFlight 下载。

如照片或草稿找不到，请保留 App 和资料，不要卸载。先确认账号与共同空间。本机手账属于当前设备，切换账号不会搬走回忆。启用过备份时，先确认最近一次备份完成，再恢复；恢复会添加副本，保留已有资料。

抠图需支持的 iPhone 与 iOS 17 或以上；模拟器不支持。选择主体清晰、光线充足、周围有留白的照片，并在深浅两种底色下检查毛绒边缘。指定主体和新编辑器仍在真机测试。

情侣与家人各用自己的账号与用户名，管理员发送邀请，对方接受后加入。情侣空间最多 2 人，家庭空间最多 10 人；成员可看到整个共同空间。管理员可分配权限或转交管理。

账号删除请进入「我的 → 隐私与账号」，或从账号邮箱联系工作室。目标 7 天内人工核实处理，需进一步核实时会联系你，并确认完成。不要发送密码。

反馈问题时请说明 App 版本、iPhone 型号、iOS 版本及出错步骤。截图请先遮挡私人照片、邮箱等信息。
